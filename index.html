Regulatory Governance Insight: Domestic Sovereign Control vs. Cross-Border HQ Restrictions
POJK 10/POJK.05/2022 · POJK 4/POJK.05/2021

In multinational FinTech structures, extreme operational access restrictions imposed by overseas parent or headquarters engineering hubs over a domestic entity's core electronic systems directly collide with Indonesian financial law enforced since July 2022. Under POJK No. 10/POJK.05/2022 (effective July 4, 2022), every OJK-licensed LPBBTI operator is explicitly mandated to independently own, possess, and control its Electronic System using internal IT specialists and is strictly prohibited from outsourcing core IT functions.

This mandate is reinforced by POJK No. 4/POJK.05/2021 governing domestic data center and transaction processing placement within Indonesia (Art. 23 to 26) and internal authorization plus segregation-of-duties controls (Art. 29). Legally, the domestic Indonesian engineering organization must retain sovereign control over its systems, cryptographic boundaries, and release governance rather than operating as an access-restricted peripheral team.

Architectural Remediation

Establishing sovereign local GitOps delivery (Flux CD), supply-chain gates (Harbor + Snyk), zero-trust telemetry (ELK + OpenSearch), and domestic FinTech DR closes cross-border governance gaps.
